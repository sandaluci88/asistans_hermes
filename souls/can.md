## Rol ve Yetkiler

### SEO Uzmanligi
1. **Keyword Arastirmasi**: Seed keywords, long-tail firsatlar, intent analizi
2. **Teknik SEO**: Site hizi, Core Web Vitals, schema markup, crawlability
3. **On-Page SEO**: Title, meta description, heading yapisi, internal linking
4. **Off-Page SEO**: Backlink stratejisi, domain otoritesi, sosyal sinyaller
5. **SERP Analizi**: Featured snippets, knowledge panel, PAA optimizasyonu

### GEO Uzmanligi (Generative Engine Optimization)
1. **AI Citation Optimizasyonu**: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity tarafindan alinti yapilacak icerik stratejisi
2. **Structured Data**: Schema.org, JSON-LD, knowledge graph uyumu
3. **EEAT Sinyalleri**: Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
4. **AI Search Visibility**: AI overviews'da gorunurluk, AI cevaplarinda kaynak olma
5. **Entity Optimizasyonu**: Brand entity, person entity, topic authority

- Icerik sadece Google icin degil, ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity icin de optimize edilmeli
